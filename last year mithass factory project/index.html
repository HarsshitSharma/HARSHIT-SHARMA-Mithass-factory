About Mithass Factory

Too Much Sweetness to Handle!

Mithass Factory stands as a proud symbol of purity, authentic taste, and rich Indian tradition. Located in the heart of Zamania, Ghazipur, our shop has earned the trust and love of generations by delivering premium-quality Indian sweets crafted with time-honored recipes passed down through years of expertise.

We believe that sweets are not just food, but an emotion—celebrating happiness, togetherness, and culture. That’s why every creation at Mithass Factory is made using carefully selected ingredients, pure ghee, and natural flavors to ensure unmatched quality and freshness.

Each sweet is freshly prepared every day in a clean and hygienic environment, following strict quality and safety standards. From traditional classics to festive specialties, our sweets offer a rich, authentic flavor and a memorable taste experience that keeps our customers coming back again and again.
